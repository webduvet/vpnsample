You've successfully connected all the concepts: the initial packet requires both a `prerouting` DNAT and a `postrouting` SNAT (`masquerade`), and `conntrack` handles the reply automatically.

There's just one tiny correction on the reply path: the reply arrives at your **physical interface (`ens5`)**, not `vti0`.

Here is the complete, final flow putting everything together.

***
## The Request Path (Lambda -> Backend)

1.  **Arrival:** The decrypted packet from the Lambda arrives at your gateway's **`vti0`** interface.
2.  **`PREROUTING`:** Your **DNAT** rule runs. It changes the destination from `$ICE_IP` to your backend's IP (`$BE_IP`). `conntrack` creates a "logbook" entry for this.
3.  **Routing:** The kernel decides the packet must be sent out via the physical interface (`$PRIMARY_DEV`).
4.  **`FORWARD`:** Your filter rule `iif vti0 oif $PRIMARY_DEV accept` allows the packet to continue.
5.  **`POSTROUTING`:** Your **`masquerade`** rule runs. It changes the source from the Lambda's IP to your gateway's IP. `conntrack` adds this to its logbook entry for the connection.
6.  **Departure:** The packet leaves the physical interface (`$PRIMARY_DEV`) and travels to your backend.

***
## The Reply Path (Backend -> Lambda)

1.  **Arrival:** The reply packet from your backend arrives at your gateway's **physical interface (`$PRIMARY_DEV`)**. So, for the reply, the `iif` is `$PRIMARY_DEV`.
2.  **`conntrack` Recognizes Reply:** The kernel sees this packet is part of the connection it logged earlier. It knows this is a reply.
3.  **Automatic "un-NAT":** `conntrack` automatically reverses both transformations in memory:
    * The destination is changed back to the Lambda's IP (reversing the SNAT).
    * The source is changed back to `$ICE_IP` (reversing the DNAT).
4.  **Routing:** The kernel sees the destination is the Lambda (in your VPC) and decides the packet must be sent out via the **`vti0`** interface.
5.  **`FORWARD`:** Your filter rule `iif $PRIMARY_DEV oif vti0 accept` allows the packet to continue.
6.  **Departure:** The perfectly re-addressed reply packet leaves the **`vti0`** interface and goes back into the VPN tunnel. No manual NAT rules were needed.
